💖 About CUTIE SNACK HOUSE

Serving happiness, one bite at a time

📖 Our Story

CUTIE SNACK HOUSE started as a small dream in the heart of Pangasinan — to bring delicious, fresh, and affordable comfort food to every table.

What began as a humble kitchen serving our favorite homemade dishes has grown into a beloved spot for family and friends to gather. Every meal we prepare is made with love, using only the freshest ingredients sourced locally whenever possible.

From our signature Grilled Chicken Gyros to our famous Somai and Steamed Momos, every dish carries the warmth of home-cooked goodness. 🏡

🎯 Our Mission

To serve delicious, high-quality food that brings people together — with fair prices, generous portions, and a smile in every order.

✨ Our Vision

To be the neighborhood's favorite place for comfort food — where everyone feels like family, and every meal is a celebration.

💎 What Makes Us Special

🌿 Fresh Daily

All our meals are prepared fresh every day — no frozen, no leftovers.

💛 Made with Love

Every recipe is crafted with care, just like how we cook for our own family.

💰 Fair Prices

Delicious food shouldn't be expensive — we keep it affordable for everyone.
